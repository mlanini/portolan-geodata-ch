# AGENTS.md — Topography & Landscape

The national topographic landscape models, geographical names, land cover, and 3D buildings that describe the physical and built landscape of Switzerland.

## Collections in this catalog

- `topography/swisstlm3d` — swissTLM3D — Topographic Landscape Model
- `topography/swisstlmregio` — swissTLMRegio — Generalized Topographic Model
- `topography/swissnames3d` — swissNAMES3D — Geographical Names
- `topography/vec200-landcover` — VECTOR200 — Land Cover
- `topography/swissbuildings3d` — swissBUILDINGS3D — 3D Building Models

All assets live under `https://ch-geodata.s3.eu-central-2.amazonaws.com/<collection-id>/` (S3: `s3://ch-geodata/<collection-id>/`).

See each collection's AGENTS.md for keys, quirks, and query recipes.
