# Topography & Landscape

> Part of the [geodata.ch](../README.md) catalog. Agents: [AGENTS.md](./AGENTS.md).

The national topographic landscape models, geographical names, land cover, and 3D buildings that describe the physical and built landscape of Switzerland.
